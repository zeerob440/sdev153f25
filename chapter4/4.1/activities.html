Activities at Pacific Trails

Hiking

Pacific Trails Resort has 5 miles of hiking trails and is adjacent to a state park.
Go it alone or join one of our guided tours.

Kayaking

Ocean Kayaks are available for guest use.

Bird Watching

While anytime is good time for bird watching at Pacific Trails, we offer guided birdwatching trips at sunrise
sevearl times a week.
